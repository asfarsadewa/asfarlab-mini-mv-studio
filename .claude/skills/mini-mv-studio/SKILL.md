# Mini MV Studio

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, then `docs/PRODUCTION.md`.
2. Run `python tools/doctor.py`. Report each FAIL.
3. Follow the steps of `docs/PRODUCTION.md` in order.

Stop at the two human gates. The owner chooses the song take by ear. The owner approves the film in the review canvas.

Tell the owner the cost before you spend on a paid service.
